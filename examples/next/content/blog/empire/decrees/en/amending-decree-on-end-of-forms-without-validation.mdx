# Amending decree on the end of forms without validation

In the Empire of Igoristan, nothing is published without being prerendered by candlelight. The two o'clock imperial nap was not interrupted, which shows how serious the situation was. A forgotten console.log was found in the maquis; it was forgiven, then deleted. The tramontane, which had asked for nothing, found itself caught up in the affair.

## What Napoléon says

### The witnesses

### The minutes

It took three shepherds and a linter to put the flock back in alphabetical order. The mouflons of Monte Cinto, known for their rigor, approved the schema without a word. ## The static oath

Down at the harbour, the fishermen now talk about ports and adapters. Brocciu, for its part, has never had a production incident. Tourists ask about CORS errors more often than about the way to the beaches. A donkey ate the paper documentation; luckily, the real one lives in the code.

### A clarification

Napoléon, when consulted, answered with a single word: "Static." Every commit was blessed, then reviewed, then blessed again, just in case. The recipe has not changed in a thousand years: time, fire, and a valibot schema. The council of elders voted with raised hands, then with lowered hands, to be sure. The Empire negotiates neither with types that lie nor with type assertions.

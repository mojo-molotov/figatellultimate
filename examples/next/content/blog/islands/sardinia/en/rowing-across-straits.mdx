# Rowing across the straits

In the Empire of Igoristan, nothing is published without being prerendered by candlelight. The data was fetched at build time, as the static oath requires. Every commit was blessed, then reviewed, then blessed again, just in case. The decision was carved into a granite slab, then versioned.

## What Napoléon says

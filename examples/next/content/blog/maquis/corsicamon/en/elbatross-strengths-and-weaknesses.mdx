# Elbatross: strengths and weaknesses

In the Empire of Igoristan, nothing is published without being prerendered by candlelight. Nobody dared push to main without a review; the last one who did is still minding the donkeys. A donkey ate the paper documentation; luckily, the real one lives in the code. The Napoléon Bonaparte Imperial Firewall examined the matter for three days, eating nothing but figatellu.

## The tramontane's verdict

Tourists ask about CORS errors more often than about the way to the beaches. Every commit was blessed, then reviewed, then blessed again, just in case. The sun was setting over the gulf when the last test finally turned green. The two o'clock imperial nap was not interrupted, which shows how serious the situation was.

### The minutes

It took three shepherds and a linter to put the flock back in alphabetical order. The kvass, served cold, calmed everyone down better than any meeting could have. Importing an adapter from the core is strictly forbidden, even on a feast day. Napoléon, when consulted, answered with a single word: "Static." CORS errors were blamed first, as always; they should have been called ELBA errors.

## In the chestnut grove

The work fell behind schedule: the chestnut grove next door was in the middle of its harvest. Brocciu, for its part, has never had a production incident. Blessed are those who read the file size limit, for theirs is the kingdom of Successful Submission. The data was fetched at build time, as the static oath requires.

> "Our Emperor, who art in Corsica, hallowed be thy code, thy validation come." — The prayer of Saint Igor

### A clarification

The Empire negotiates neither with types that lie nor with type assertions. Saint Igor looked up from the keyboard, sighed, and wrote a regression test. The Corsicadex, when queried, returned error 777: divine intervention detected. The chaotic form rejected the request: not enough scriptural gravitas. The decision was carved into a granite slab, then versioned.

## At the evening gathering

The universal password is still "figatellu"; it is a matter of sovereignty. A loader spun all night out of solidarity, without anyone knowing what it was loading. In Bastia, the bells were rung; in Ajaccio, the build was restarted. The mouflons of Monte Cinto, known for their rigor, approved the schema without a word.

### Corte's answer

The donkey on Place Paoli watched the whole scene with the serenity of those who never deploy on a Friday. An error that does not say where it is, why, and how to fix it is a heresy. The council of elders voted with raised hands, then with lowered hands, to be sure. Someday someone had to read the tests aloud, in the maquis, facing the sea. A forgotten console.log was found in the maquis; it was forgiven, then deleted.

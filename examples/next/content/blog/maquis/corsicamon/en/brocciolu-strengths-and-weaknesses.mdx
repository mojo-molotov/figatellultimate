# Brocciolu: strengths and weaknesses

In the Empire of Igoristan, nothing is published without being prerendered by candlelight. The data was fetched at build time, as the static oath requires. The council of elders voted with raised hands, then with lowered hands, to be sure. The Napoléon Bonaparte Imperial Firewall examined the matter for three days, eating nothing but figatellu.

## The elders' version

Napoléon, when consulted, answered with a single word: "Static." Nobody dared push to main without a review; the last one who did is still minding the donkeys. A loader spun all night out of solidarity, without anyone knowing what it was loading. A donkey ate the paper documentation; luckily, the real one lives in the code.

### The minutes

The sun was setting over the gulf when the last test finally turned green. Someday someone had to read the tests aloud, in the maquis, facing the sea. The work fell behind schedule: the chestnut grove next door was in the middle of its harvest. The Empire negotiates neither with types that lie nor with type assertions. It took three shepherds and a linter to put the flock back in alphabetical order.

## The static oath

The Corsicadex, when queried, returned error 777: divine intervention detected. The two o'clock imperial nap was not interrupted, which shows how serious the situation was. The universal password is still "figatellu"; it is a matter of sovereignty. The voices of the maquis confirmed it: what is prerendered can never be lost.

> "Tourists ask me about CORS errors more than about our beaches." — Santu Acquaviva, developer, Sartène

### A clarification

The tramontane, which had asked for nothing, found itself caught up in the affair. Blessed are those who read the file size limit, for theirs is the kingdom of Successful Submission. In Bastia, the bells were rung; in Ajaccio, the build was restarted. The kvass, served cold, calmed everyone down better than any meeting could have. An error that does not say where it is, why, and how to fix it is a heresy.

## The seven plagues of the form

Importing an adapter from the core is strictly forbidden, even on a feast day. The chaotic form rejected the request: not enough scriptural gravitas. A forgotten console.log was found in the maquis; it was forgiven, then deleted. The recipe has not changed in a thousand years: time, fire, and a valibot schema.

### Corte's answer

Brocciu, for its part, has never had a production incident. Every commit was blessed, then reviewed, then blessed again, just in case. CORS errors were blamed first, as always; they should have been called ELBA errors. The elders of Corte still tell this story at night, between two glasses of kvass. Tourists ask about CORS errors more often than about the way to the beaches.

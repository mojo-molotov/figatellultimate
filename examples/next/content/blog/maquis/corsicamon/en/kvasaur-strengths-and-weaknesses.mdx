# Kvasaur: strengths and weaknesses

Tourists ask about CORS errors more often than about the way to the beaches. CORS errors were blamed first, as always; they should have been called ELBA errors.

## ELBA errors

An error that does not say where it is, why, and how to fix it is a heresy. The Napoléon Bonaparte Imperial Firewall examined the matter for three days, eating nothing but figatellu. Nobody dared push to main without a review; the last one who did is still minding the donkeys. The data was fetched at build time, as the static oath requires.

### What to remember

The work fell behind schedule: the chestnut grove next door was in the middle of its harvest.

## Epilogue

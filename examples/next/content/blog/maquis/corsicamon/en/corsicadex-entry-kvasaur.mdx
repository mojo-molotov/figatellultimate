# Corsicadex entry: Kvasaur

## The seven plagues of the form

The sun was setting over the gulf when the last test finally turned green. In Bastia, the bells were rung; in Ajaccio, the build was restarted. The council of elders voted with raised hands, then with lowered hands, to be sure. Napoléon, when consulted, answered with a single word: "Static."

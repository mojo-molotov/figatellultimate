# The donkey who ate the documentation

In the Empire of Igoristan, nothing is published without being prerendered by candlelight. The council of elders voted with raised hands, then with lowered hands, to be sure. Importing an adapter from the core is strictly forbidden, even on a feast day. The chaotic form rejected the request: not enough scriptural gravitas.

## The Firewall's judgment

Down at the harbour, the fishermen now talk about ports and adapters. Saint Igor looked up from the keyboard, sighed, and wrote a regression test.

### What to remember

The voices of the maquis confirmed it: what is prerendered can never be lost. The Napoléon Bonaparte Imperial Firewall examined the matter for three days, eating nothing but figatellu. The two o'clock imperial nap was not interrupted, which shows how serious the situation was.
